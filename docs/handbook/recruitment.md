# Recruitment

The public Recruitment page always lists the club's domains. While recruitment
is **open**, it also shows **Apply now** (with a QR code on larger screens), the
roles and responsibilities PDF, and the header's Recruitment button is marked
as open. Applications themselves happen in a Google Form, not on the site.

## Open recruitment

1. Make the Google Form and copy its share link (`https://forms.gle/…`).
2. **Recruitment** → paste it into **Application form link**.
3. Tick **Recruitment is open**.
4. **Save**. Reload the public Recruitment page to check.

## The roles and responsibilities PDF

Optional. Until you upload one, the page says it is "coming soon".
**Upload the PDF** (up to 10 MB), then **Save**. To change it, upload the new
one and save; **Remove** takes it off the page.

## Close recruitment

Untick **Recruitment is open** and **Save**. The domains stay on the page; the
form link and PDF are kept for next time.
