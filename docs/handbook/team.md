# Team

The About page shows the **core team** (the office-bearers) first, then the
**domain secretaries** — one for each domain. Coordinators and volunteers are
not listed.

## Add someone

1. **Team** → **Add member**.
2. **Name**, and **Part of**: _Core team_ or _Domain secretaries_.
3. For a secretary, choose the **Domain they lead**; the **Position** fills in
   as "Secretary, …" if it is empty. For the core team, write the position:
   President, Vice President, Treasurer…
4. **Photo** (optional): a clear head-and-shoulders photo. It is shown as a
   square cropped from the centre. Without one, the card shows their initials.
5. **Add member**. They join the end of their group.

## Put the core team in order

On the **Team** page, use the ↑ and ↓ arrows beside each core-team member.
Each move is saved at once. Secretaries are always shown in the order of their
domains, so they have no arrows.

## Change or remove someone

Click their name, change anything, **Save changes**. **Remove member** takes
them off the About page and deletes their photo; it asks first.

## When the committee changes

Remove last year's members and add the new ones. The Team page lists the
domains that have no secretary yet.
