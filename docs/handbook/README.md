# SIG Reality Spectra — admin handbook

For the club's coordinators: how to keep the website up to date. You do not
need to know anything about code. Everything here is done in the browser, in
the **admin panel**.

## Signing in

1. Go to **`https://<club-domain>/admin`**. There is no link to it anywhere on
   the site, on purpose; bookmark it.
2. Click **Sign in with Google** and choose your club Google account.
3. Only the accounts on the admin list can get in. If yours is refused, ask
   whoever looks after the website to add it ([admins.md](./admins.md)).

You stay signed in for 8 hours. **Sign out** is at the bottom of the menu.

Every save shows on the public site **straight away** — reload the public page
to see it. Nothing needs to be "published" or "deployed".

## Good to know

- **Two people editing the same thing:** if someone saved it after you opened
  it, your save is refused with a "Someone else saved this" message, so nobody
  overwrites anyone. Click **Reload**, then make your change again.
- **Leaving with unsaved changes:** the panel asks before you lose them.
- **Photos:** any shape is fine. They are shrunk and converted in your browser
  before uploading, so phone photos upload quickly. Always describe each
  photo in a few words (the "alt text") — it is read aloud to people who
  cannot see it, and helps search engines.
- **Mistakes:** every night the site backs up all its content, and keeps 30
  days of backups. If something important is deleted by accident, tell the
  person who looks after the website the same week ([backups.md](./backups.md)).
